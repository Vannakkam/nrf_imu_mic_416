Seeed nRF52840 Sense — BLE Recorder

Web Bluetooth recorder: IMU at 416 Hz (24 samples / packet, 12-bit packed) and microphone audio at 8 kHz (IMA ADPCM). Batching keeps the BLE packet rate at ~37/s instead of ~58/s.

1. Connection

Status: Disconnected

Requires Chrome/Edge on Android or desktop (Web Bluetooth). Page must be served over HTTPS or localhost.

2–3. Recording

Host system time is sent to the device on connect and again on every Start, so absolute timestamps survive Reset/Stop/Start. Device-reported loss counters appear in the log via STAT and on Stop.
